This policy explains what data the Classic Marbles app processes, why, and how.

In short

  • The game is played without an internet connection. Your save data stays only on your phone.
  • The internet is used for four features only: ads, the Premium purchase, playing with a friend far away using a Room Code, and feedback that you choose to send.
  • You do not need an account. We never ask for your real name, email address, phone number or location.
  • We do not sell your data. The app contains no analytics or tracking tools.

Data that stays only on your device

DataWhy
The player name you type, your avatar, and other player names entered for pass-and-play matchesTo show them in the game
Your marble collection, unlocked marbles, ad unlock progress, match statisticsTo let you continue where you left off
Your settings (language, sound, vibration, graphics, field)To remember your preferences

This information stays in the app's own folder and is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Classic Marbles is free. To keep developing it, the app shows Google AdMob ads:

  • One app open ad when you launch the app. It is not shown on the very first launch or when you are offline, and never after you buy Premium.
  • Rewarded ads that you choose to watch to unlock a marble.

To show ads, Google AdMob collects and processes the following from your device: advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information (model, operating system, language), and ad interactions (impressions, clicks). Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, show personalized ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalized ads are shown only with your consent. The consent form appears on first launch; you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options in the game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google > Ads).
  • Ads are limited to age-appropriate content (maximum ad content rating PG). Gambling, dating and similar sensitive categories are blocked.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases (Google Play)

The Premium pack is bought through Google Play. We never see your payment details (card number etc.); Google processes the payment. The app only learns from Google Play that the purchase is valid and unlocks Premium. The purchase is tied to your Google account and comes back when you reinstall the app.

Playing with a Room Code

If you choose to play with a friend far away using a 6-digit room code, the following is sent to Google Firebase servers (Europe, Belgium) to match you and run the match:

  • A randomly generated anonymous ID. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account.
  • The player name you typed, the marble you picked and the match settings.
  • Your shots during the match.

These records are used only for matchmaking and the match itself. If you cancel before the match starts they are deleted immediately; everything else is cleaned up within 30 days at the latest.

Feedback

If you choose to report a problem via Feedback in Settings, the following is sent:

  • The description you write.
  • A screenshot of the game screen at that moment. You can remove it by tapping the image before sending.
  • Phone model, operating system, graphics card, screen resolution, memory size, app version, current screen and language.
  • The game's last technical log lines (error messages).
  • The anonymous ID.

This information is used only to find and fix bugs and is stored on Google Firebase servers (Europe, Belgium). We delete it from the server after reviewing it. Please do not include personal information in your description.

Children

Classic Marbles is designed for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you notice that such data has been collected, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (Europe), Turkish KVKK and the laws of some US states (e.g. California), you have the right to be informed about, access, correct and delete your data, to object to its processing and to withdraw your consent.

  • For data on our server (Room Code, feedback), write to ridsgames.destek@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days at the latest.
  • For data on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing its data is enough.
  • For ad data, use the advertising ID settings in Android and Settings > Privacy options in the game. In the US you can also opt out of sharing your data for personalized ads there.

Ridsgame is the data controller. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.
